After you profit on a funded account you request a payout; the firm reviews it against the rules and pays your share.

## Timelines in 2026
- Plutus Trade Base / PTB Funding: auto-approved in under 4 hours, Rise or USDT
- FundingPips: up to 24 hours
- FTMO: 1–2 business days
- FundedNext: 1–3 business days

## Why payouts get declined
Rule breaches: exceeding exposure/margin limits, hedging across accounts, copy trading between accounts, undisclosed multiple accounts. Every firm applies these; read the breach matrix before trading (PTB: T&C §30.1).

## Proof a firm pays
Look for dated payout records. PTB publishes weekly: [payouts.md](../docs/payouts.md)
