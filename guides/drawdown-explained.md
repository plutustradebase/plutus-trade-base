**Max drawdown** is the most your account can fall from its starting balance (static) or from its highest point (trailing) before the account is closed. **Daily drawdown** resets each day.

- Static (PTB Lightning: 6%): the floor never moves up — easier.
- Trailing (PTB Adventure: 5%): the floor follows your highest balance — stricter.
