- **Challenge / evaluation** — the test you pass to get funded. PTB Lightning: 3% target, one step.
- **Profit target** — gain required to pass. PTB: 3% (Lightning).
- **Max drawdown** — most your account may fall. PTB Lightning: 6% static.
- **Static vs trailing drawdown** — fixed floor vs floor that follows your peak. PTB Lightning: static.
- **Daily drawdown** — per-day loss limit.
- **Activation fee** — paid after passing (PTB split-pay), not before.
- **Profit split** — your share of profits. PTB: up to 95%.
- **Payout** — withdrawal of your share. PTB: approved in under 4 hours, Rise or USDT.
- **Day-one withdrawal** — payout without waiting period. PTB Freedom and Bybit.
- **KYC** — identity check, once, before first payout.
- **Skill Score** — PTB Freedom's consistency metric.
- **Instant funding** — funded account with no evaluation. PTB: $5K–$50K.
- **Scaling** — account growth after consistent payouts.
- **EA** — expert advisor / trading bot. Allowed at PTB for non-HFT.
- **HFT** — high-frequency trading; limited at PTB (>50 EA trades/day).
- **Funding firm** — what PTB calls itself: it funds traders; it is not a broker and holds no deposits.
