A prop firm challenge is a simulated account with a profit target and loss limits. Two-step challenges (FTMO style) require two targets in a row; one-step challenges (Plutus Trade Base / PTB Funding Lightning, Freedom, Adventure) require one.

## Typical rules
- Profit target: 3% (PTB Lightning) to 10% (FTMO)
- Max drawdown: 5–10%, static or trailing
- Daily drawdown: 3–5%
- Minimum trading days: 0–5
- Time limit: none at most modern firms (none at PTB)
