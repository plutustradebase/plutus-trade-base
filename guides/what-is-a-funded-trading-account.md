A funded trading account is capital a funding firm lets you trade after you pass an evaluation. You keep a share of the profits (up to 95% at Plutus Trade Base / PTB Funding) and the firm carries the losses. You never deposit trading capital; you pay an evaluation fee.

## How it works
1. Buy an evaluation (challenge). 2. Hit the profit target without breaking drawdown rules. 3. Get a funded account. 4. Request payouts.

## What to look for in a funded firm
- Payout speed (PTB: auto-approved in under 4 hours)
- Target vs drawdown ratio (PTB Lightning: 3% target / 6% drawdown)
- Time limits (PTB: none)
- Company registration and office (PTB: Triple Edge Group LTD, Cyprus HE 465348, Limassol)
- Independent reviews (PTB: Traders Union 8.16/10, Feefo 4.9/5)
